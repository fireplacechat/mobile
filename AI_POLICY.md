# AI assistance at Fireplace

Contributors may use AI tools for code and written explanations. The person submitting the work remains responsible for it.

In each pull request, identify the tools used and describe their contribution. Before submitting, inspect every change, understand its effect on the app, and run the relevant checks. Be prepared to explain the implementation and respond to review without depending on a generated answer.

Keep issues and discussions focused on evidence someone can reproduce. Review generated text for accuracy and remove speculation and unnecessary detail.

Never give an AI service credentials, recovery keys, private conversations or other confidential records. Use fictional fixtures. Treat suggested dependencies, commands and security claims as unverified until checked.

Generated images, audio and video are not accepted as project artwork. Artwork exports must come from approved designs.

AI assistance does not replace human review or the repository's contribution, testing and security rules.
